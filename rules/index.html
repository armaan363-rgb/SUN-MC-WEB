Rules

Play fair and everyone enjoys the sunrise. Breaking these rules can lead to punishment.

🚫 Bad Words, Abuse & Toxicity

Keep chat clean and friendly for every player.

No abusive language

Swearing, insulting, or using offensive words toward other players is not allowed.

No disguised abuse

Using short forms, censored words, symbols, numbers, or intentionally misspelled words to bypass the rules is also considered abuse.

Examples:lmcbcb*cf*@#$%or similar variations

No personal attacks

Do not insult someone's family, friends, appearance, gameplay, or personal life.

No harassment

Repeatedly targeting, provoking, annoying, or verbally abusing another player is prohibited.

No toxic behavior

Excessive trash talk, disrespect, threats, or deliberately creating arguments in chat is not allowed.

No abusive nicknames/usernames

Offensive, hateful, or inappropriate names and nicknames may result in punishment.

Do not bypass chat filters

Trying to avoid the server's chat filter by adding spaces, symbols, numbers, or changing spellings will still count as breaking this rule.

Respect everyone

Keep the server friendly and enjoyable for all players. Jokes are allowed, but they must not become offensive or targeted harassment.

⚠️ Punishments

Depending on the severity and frequency:

  1. Warning
  2. Mute
  3. Longer Mute
  4. Kick
  5. Temporary Ban
  6. Permanent Ban

Staff may apply a stronger punishment for severe or repeated abuse.
